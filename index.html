About Hitch

Hitch 0.1 · payment channels in a browser tab, for txbt4, the BLAKE2b testnet4.

The Lightning shape without the network: a 2-of-2 funding output, asymmetric commitments with a revocable to_local, penalties for old states, cooperative and forced closes. Funded, watched and settled by the tab's own node; messages between tabs over the relays. Not compatible with Lightning peers, and not private: channel messages are signed, not encrypted.

Source · AGPL-3.0-or-later.

The channel documents hold the signed commitments and the revocation secrets, not the key. Losing them means the other side can close at any signed state and cannot be punished for an old one; export after every session that changed something, and keep the file where only you can read it. Never restore an old copy over a newer one: a channel restored at an old state and force-closed is a cheat, and is punished.
